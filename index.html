Our Heritage

The Story Behind Kingdom of Snowstorm

I am Katerina Gerasimovska, founder and owner of Kingdom of Snowstorm, an FCI-registered Bichon Frisé kennel based in Skopje, North Macedonia.

The story of Kingdom of Snowstorm began in 2016, with a little white Bichon Frisé named Hannah who came into my life and changed it in a way I could have never imagined.

Today, at 10 years old, Hannah still holds a very special place in my heart. She is the first page of the story I continue to write.

With time, Hannah’s story continued through her daughter Galla, and later through Galla’s daughter Darla. Through them, the story that began with one little Bichon became a part of something much bigger.

As Kingdom of Snowstorm continued to grow, this year brought Oslo into our family – Petit Ami’s Take on Me, from the respected Petit Ami kennel in Oslo, Norway.

"The name Kingdom of Snowstorm was born from the special world I share with my Bichons. They are my little kingdom – the ones who made this name come to life. Like a snowstorm, they bring movement, energy, and an unforgettable feeling to every day."
