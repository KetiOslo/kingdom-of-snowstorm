Our Heritage

The Story Behind Kingdom of Snowstorm

I am Katerina Gerasimovska, founder and owner of Kingdom of Snowstorm, an FCI-registered Bichon Frisé kennel based in Skopje, North Macedonia.

The story of Kingdom of Snowstorm began in 2016, with a little white Bichon Frisé named Hannah who came into my life and changed it in a way I could have never imagined. She was not only my first Bichon — she was the beginning of a beautiful connection with this wonderful breed and the reason a simple love for one little dog slowly became a lifelong journey.

Today, at 10 years old, Hannah still holds a very special place in my heart. She is the first page of the story I continue to write, the one who brought me into the world of Bichon Frisé and inspired everything that came after her.

With time, Hannah’s story continued through her daughter Galla, and later through Galla’s daughter Darla. Through them, the story that began with one little Bichon became a part of something much bigger, carrying the same love from one generation to the next.

As Kingdom of Snowstorm continued to grow, I felt that a new chapter was ready to begin. This year, that chapter brought Oslo into our family — Petit Ami’s Take on Me, from the respected Petit Ami kennel in Oslo, Norway. His arrival became part of the ongoing story of my kennel and the future I continue to build with my dogs.

"The name Kingdom of Snowstorm was born from the special world I share with my Bichons. They are my little kingdom — the ones who made this name come to life. Like a snowstorm, they bring movement, energy, and an unforgettable feeling to every day."
